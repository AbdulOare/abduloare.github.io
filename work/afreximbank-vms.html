Afreximbank · 2024 to present

Vendor Management System: a 129-page user manual and quick guides

Vendors across Africa register, get certified, submit work and get paid through Afreximbank's Vendor Management System. I wrote the documentation that walks them through it.

RoleDocumentation Engineer
EngagementContract via Revent Technologies
AudienceExternal vendors
FormatsManual, quick guides, video

The brief

129page vendor user manual (v1.2)
11page SOW quick guide
6SOW stages, setup to paid
3formats: manual, guide, video

The VMS covers the full vendor lifecycle: invitation, registration, KYC, re-certification, work requests, and the Statement of Work, timesheet and invoice module. Vendors use it without training, so the documentation has to do the training.

What I did

  • Wrote the Vendor User Manual: 129 pages of step-by-step, screenshot-led tasks, from receiving an invitation to resubmitting KYC data and confirming payment.
  • Condensed the Statement of Work module into an 11-page quick guide that shows the whole flow, from resource setup to Paid, on one page before going step by step.
  • Produced video walkthroughs to go with the written guides.
  • Kept the manual current through product phases (v1.2 adds the Phase 3 update).
